# EMUAS-Aneris
Expandable Multi-imaging Underwater Acquisition System 

Underwater observation is a vital part of underwater research and can be a great tool to increase public engagement in underwater preservation. Underwater cameras can be used in order to monitor biodiversity over time and the effectiveness of different environmental measures. The different solutions  that are available on the market for long-term and continuous observation are expensive. The EMUAS was developed as a low-cost solution in order to increase the availability of good data for researchers and the public. The unit developed at OsloMet Oceanlab is part of a bigger EU project called Aneris. Aneris aims to increase underwater research and environmental impact studies.
